If you couldnt tell, i LOVE mushrooms. Aside from being a slightly controversial food ingredient, mushrooms actually have a lot of potential for bio-remediation and technology, because they have exceptional potential for electrical conductivity, as well as being able to remove several pollutants including heavy metals and pharmaceutical pollutants! Also, even after having foraged, grown and eaten over ten different types of mushrooms, i am never short of surprised by the sheer amount of textures, flavors, and colors mushrooms can have! My personal favorite right now is chantrelle mushrooms, which i found in a forest. They have a much softer, butterier texture than portobello mushrooms, and have a mild fruity, mushroomy flavor that works well in cream soup!

Since i was little, i have always loved foraging wild raspberries and wild blackberries, because they were easy to identify, and most importantly - they were delicious! Over several years, i started foraging more, and now i can identify many plants without Google Lens, including several species of mushrooms, berries, herbs, trees, and flowers!
